# BAIWSAQ public site status

- The official $BAIWSAQ CA and six AI-stock CAs are independent database records.
  An open market or detail page polls them each second. Publishing an address
  does not rebuild GitHub Pages.
- The home `02 / PLATFORM MARKET` panel switches from TBA to the published CA,
  a DexScreener embed and a token link on the next poll. Live candles depend on
  a real trading pair being indexed by DexScreener.
- Solana Wallet Standard discovery, account connection and disconnection work
  without the retired authentication service. The site does not request a
  signature just to connect.
- Coin creation, reward payouts, activity history and protocol buybacks are
  prelaunch. The public Pages site has no production launch transaction backend
  or verified on-chain reward service. The launch form is a disabled preview.
- The public routes use the six AI company names. Old company URLs redirect to
  their replacements. The retired project domain is not used by the Pages site.
